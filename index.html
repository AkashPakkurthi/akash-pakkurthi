Side project · open source

AI-powered distributed search engine

  • Async aiohttp crawlers ingest pages in parallel on FastAPI, Celery, Redis and SQLAlchemy.
  • Hybrid retrieval fuses TF-IDF and all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings (FAISS) with Reciprocal Rank Fusion.
  • A GPT-4o-mini RAG layer rewrites queries and synthesizes answers from the top-K results.
  • A Redis result cache cuts repeat-query latency from ~2s to under 10ms and clears itself on every reindex.
  • A provider-agnostic LLM layer works with OpenAI, Groq or Ollama through one base-URL setting.
